Two tools

The skill

Instructions that make Claude a careful assistant on a film:

  • upload the right references
  • make cheap drafts
  • check every take against your continuity rules
  • finish in 1080p, upscale to 4K, master in ProRes 4444
  • render the cut, one file per shot plus the joined cut

The edit bench

A single web page, with no install and no upload: your clips stay on your computer.

  • open a folder of takes
  • add any take in any order
  • trim each one to the frame
  • play the cut end to end
  • export an EDL (CMX3600), a CSV or a project file

Use the edit bench

  1. Open the edit bench in Chrome, Safari or Firefox. You can also download edit-bench/index.html and open it offline.
  2. Click Open folder and pick your takes, or drop video files onto the page.
    • Takes are grouped by subfolder, or by the letters their name starts with (A1, A2 → A).
    • An optional bench.json can name the groups and describe takes: see the example.
  3. Click Add on the takes you want. Then reorder them, select a row to trim it, and play the cut.
  4. Save named versions as you go, then export the EDL for your editing software, or the CSV for the render script.

Browsers play MP4 (H.264) everywhere and MOV in Safari. For ProRes masters, make light previews first with scripts/make_preview.sh. Your edit and versions are kept in your browser; after a reload, open the folder again so the clips can play.

Use the skill

  1. Get Claude, plus a paid Higgsfield and/or Magnific plan, and add them to Claude as connectors.
    • Higgsfield: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp
    • Magnific: https://mcp.magnific.com
    • Full steps: setup guide.
  2. Zip the ai-film-shots folder and add it to Claude as a skill.
  3. In a new chat: “Use the ai-film-shots skill. Help me write the continuity file for my film.”

Scripts

They need ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg on a Mac).

contact_sheet.shOne image with stamped frames of a take, to judge it at a glance
encode_prores4444.shProRes 4444 master, optionally mirrored (a wound on the wrong side) and conformed to the frame size
render_cut.shFrame-accurate shots from the edit bench's CSV, then the joined cut, in ProRes, 4K MP4 or a light preview
make_preview.shLight preview and thumbnail of a take, for the edit bench
